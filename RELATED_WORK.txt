Primary sources checked while developing the experiment

SET: Mocanu et al. (2018), Nature Communications.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-04316-3
Erdos-Renyi initialization and evolutionary sparse connectivity are prior work.

RigL: Evci et al. (2020), ICML.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/evci20a.html
Gradient-informed sparse regrowth is prior work. gradient_set in this package is
inspired by this principle, not an exact reproduction of RigL.

Structured sparsity: Wen et al. (2016), NeurIPS.
https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2016/hash/41bfd20a38bb1b0bec75acf0845530a7-Abstract.html
Group regularization and compact structure learning are prior work.

SRigL / Dynamic Sparse Training with Structured Sparsity (ICLR 2024).
https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/8c5f30296296d2ae402ebbd09aaa9c12-Abstract-Conference.html
Combining dynamic sparse training and neuron/structured pruning is prior work.
This is an important comparison before claiming methodological originality.

Advancing Dynamic Sparse Training by Exploring Optimization Opportunities (ICML 2024).
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/ji24a.html
The dynamic sparse training literature has substantial recent developments;
SET-only comparisons do not establish state-of-the-art performance.

UK-DALE: Kelly and Knottenbelt (2015), Scientific Data.
https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata20157
Raw measurements and household provenance are necessary for source-independent
NILM claims. The supplied CSVs contain synthetic windows and binary labels.

This is a focused starting review, not an exhaustive originality search. The
candidate appliance-specific validation controller and structured combination
must be evaluated against this and broader prior work before a novelty claim.
